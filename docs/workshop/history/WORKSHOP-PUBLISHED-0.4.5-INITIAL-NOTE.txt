0.4.5 - Borrowed gear and construction fixes

- Clear stale borrowed-item tags after outfit returns and when loading existing saves. Active borrowed gear, saved personal items and selected or retained locker stock remain protected.
- Fix automatic installation pickups interrupted by outfit preparation when the pawn drops the single minified building.
- Prevent builders from selecting floor-removal work already claimed by another pawn preparing its outfit.
- Let blueprint-triggered floor removal resume after outfit preparation. Removed or replaced blueprints safely cancel the old continuation.

Existing saves are supported; rule defaults are unchanged. An older save's already-pending floor job may be released for normal job selection because it lacks the new blueprint identity.
